# Key Concepts: How To Train Tendons As Brain — 2026-06-01

## Tendon Physiology and Mechanics

- **Tendon Structure**: Tendons are composed of wavy collagen fibres that straighten under load, with increased cross-links between fibres providing greater elasticity and stiffness.
- **Force-Deformation Curve**: The tendon force-deformation curve has three regions: toe (slack removal), linear (spring-like energy storage at ~6–8% deformation), and ultimate failure (damage that spurs remodelling).
- **Musculotendinous Unit**: The functional system combining muscle as the motor and tendon as the spring for coordinated force production and energy storage.

## Training Progression and Adaptation

- **Three-Phase Training Blueprint**: A structured approach requiring concurrent development of strength, power, and tendon adaptation without skipping phases to maximize performance gains.
- **Long-Term Consistency**: Sustained training over years rather than months is the differentiator between athletes who plateau and those who achieve record-breaking results.

## Performance Development Mindset

- **Genetic Influence with Controllable Improvement**: While genetics influences jumping ability, improvement is always within your control regardless of starting point.
- **Plateau Resilience**: Plateaus are normal training phases that often precede significant breakthroughs, and success requires continuing effort rather than stopping.
